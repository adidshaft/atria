# Atria Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

Atria is an open-source iPhone app that reads a WHOOP 4.0 strap over Bluetooth
and computes sleep, recovery, strain and related wellness estimates **on your
iPhone**. Atria has no account, no server and no analytics. The developer does
not receive, collect, sell or share any of your data.

## What Atria stores, and where

Everything below stays in Atria's storage on your iPhone. It is removed when
you delete the app.

| Data | Why | Leaves the phone? |
|---|---|---|
| Heart rate, beat-to-beat intervals, motion, battery and history records from your strap (Bluetooth) | To compute your metrics | No |
| Profile you enter (age, optional sex, weight, height, max HR) | To personalise estimates | No |
| Journal answers, sleep and workout confirmations, notes | To show trends you asked for | No |
| Workout route (Location), only while an outdoor workout you started is active | To show route, distance and pace | No |
| Motion activity (Core Motion) | To avoid counting driving as exercise | No |
| A photo you pick for a share card (Camera/Photos) | To place behind that one card | No |

## Things that happen only when you choose them

- **Apple Health.** If you turn on export, Atria writes heart rate, workouts
  and confirmed sleep to Apple Health, and reads back only what it needs to
  confirm the export. If you turn on nutrition context, it reads nutrition
  samples. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves the
  device through Atria.
- **Exports, backups and research bundles.** These are files you create and
  send yourself through the iOS share sheet. Atria does not upload them.
- **Links.** "Sources" and help links open in your browser. That website's own
  policy then applies.
- **Coach.** Questions go to Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to a
  server.
- **Maps.** Workout maps use Apple MapKit. Apple may receive map-tile requests
  under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

Atria is not directed to children under 13.

## Not medical software

Atria gives wellness estimates. It is not a medical device and does not
diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.
